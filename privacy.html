ReRoll Privacy Policy

Effective 6 September 2026. ReRoll is operated by PJ Ogilvie ("we", "us"). This policy explains what ReRoll does with information when you use it, including when you connect a TikTok account.

What ReRoll is

ReRoll is a desktop application that generates anime wheel-spin videos and, if you choose, posts them to your own TikTok account through TikTok's official Content Posting API.

Information we receive from TikTok

When you connect a TikTok account, TikTok gives ReRoll an access token together with your TikTok open id, username, display name and avatar, and the posting options TikTok allows for your account (for example which privacy levels you may post with and whether comments, duets and stitches are enabled). ReRoll uses this only to show you which account a post will go to, to present the posting options TikTok requires, and to upload the videos you choose.

Where it is stored

ReRoll runs on your own computer. The access token and refresh token are stored in a file on that computer, readable only by your user account. They are never sent anywhere except to TikTok's API. We do not operate a server that receives your token, your videos or your account details.

What we do not do

  • We do not sell, share or transfer your TikTok data to third parties.
  • We do not read your messages, your followers, your analytics or any content on your account.
  • We do not post anything you did not explicitly review and confirm in the app.
  • We do not add watermarks or promotional text to your videos.

Videos you post

Videos are generated on your computer from the wheel results and uploaded directly to TikTok when you confirm. Once posted, a video is governed by TikTok's own terms and privacy policy.

Comment requests

Creators using ReRoll may spin lives for people who leave a comment under one of their TikTok videos. In that case ReRoll reads the public comment text and the commenter's public username so the creator can mention them in the resulting caption. Nothing else about the commenter is collected, and a commenter can ask for a video to be removed by contacting us.

Deleting your data

Revoke ReRoll's access at any time in TikTok under Settings and privacy, Security, Manage app permissions. Deleting the token file on your computer, or uninstalling ReRoll, removes everything ReRoll holds about your TikTok account.

Children

ReRoll is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect information from them.
