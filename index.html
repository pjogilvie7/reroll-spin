ReRoll

Spin the wheel. Watch a whole anime life get rolled. Post it to TikTok.

ReRoll is a wheel-spin video generator for anime fans and creators. One seed decides everything: which world a character is born into, their clan or school, their power, the fights they win, the ones they lose, and how the story ends. ReRoll renders the whole life as a vertical video, with reveal cards, music and a caption, and lets you post it straight to your own TikTok account.

How it works

1. Connect TikTokSign in with TikTok. ReRoll only asks for permission to post videos you choose, on the account you pick. You can revoke it any time in TikTok's settings.
2. SpinPick a verse, or let the Multiverse wheel choose one. The wheel rolls a name, an origin, a power, a rank and a fate, and ReRoll renders it.
3. ReviewWatch the finished video, edit the caption, choose who can see it and whether comments, duets and stitches are allowed.
4. PostConfirm, and ReRoll uploads the video to TikTok. Processing takes a few minutes and the app shows you the status until it is live.

Verses

Naruto, Jujutsu Kaisen, Chainsaw Man, Bleach, Demon Slayer, My Hero Academia, Black Clover, Solo Leveling and the Multiverse wheel that picks among them. New verses are added as their engines pass the ship gate.

Requests

Creators can also spin lives for the people who comment on their videos. Drop a name under a ReRoll post and the next spin can be yours.

Get ReRoll

ReRoll runs on your own computer as a desktop app and connects to TikTok with TikTok's official Login Kit and Content Posting API. Source and releases are on GitHub.
